三条规则Three rules

你的真实理财习惯，就是你的角色数值。Your real money habits are your character's stats.

⭐ 经验 = 纪律XP = Discipline

每天记账 +10，连续 7 天 +50，本周比上周少花 +100，一个月存下两成以上 +200。经验只增不减。

Log every day +10, a 7-day streak +50, spend less than last week +100, save 20% of the month +200. XP never goes down.

⚔️ 攻击 = 资产Attack = Savings

资产越多，伤害越高；但成长会放慢，所以小资产的新手也打得赢。

More net worth means harder hits, with growth that tapers off, so newer players can still win.

❤️ 血量 = 预算Health = Budget

血条就是本月预算还剩多少。月底前花光，上竞技场就危险了。

Your health bar is how much of this month's budget is left. Run it dry and the arena gets risky.
